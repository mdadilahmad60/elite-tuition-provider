Jharkhand Skill Development

Free Skill Training Opportunities

Information for eligible Jharkhand candidates under government-supported skill development programs.

For eligible Jharkhand residents

Learn Skills with Support

Under Mukhyamantri Sarthi Yojana / Jharkhand Skill Development Mission Society, eligible candidates may access approved training programs with support benefits.

Age mentioned: 18–35 years. Eligibility and available courses depend on current scheme rules and authorised training centres.

Training Areas May Include

  • Computer and office skills
  • Fashion designing and sewing machine operator
  • General Duty Assistant (healthcare)
  • Food & Beverage Service Assistant
  • Call Centre Executive
  • Other approved short-term skill courses

Possible Support Benefits

  • Training fee support for eligible candidates
  • Accommodation and meals where approved
  • Books and training dress where included
  • Certificate after completing course requirements
  • Placement assistance as per program rules

Placement and salary are not guaranteed. Benefits, districts and course availability must be confirmed with an authorised centre.
